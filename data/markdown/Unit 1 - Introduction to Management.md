1. Management: Concept, Meaning, and 
Functions 
Concept of Management 
Management is a process through which managers coordinate and utilize organizational 
resources efficiently to achieve predetermined goals. 
These resources include: 
● Human resources (employees)
 
● Financial resources (money)
 
● Physical resources (machines, buildings)
 
● Information and technology
 
Management ensures that all resources work together effectively. 
In simple terms: 
Management means getting work done through people in an organized manner. 
 
Meaning of Management 
Management is both: 
1. A Process 
It involves a series of activities such as planning, organizing, directing, and controlling. 
2. An Activity

# Page 2

Managers guide employees and coordinate organizational work. 
3. A Discipline 
Management is also studied as an academic subject in business and technology programs. 
 
Characteristics of Management 
1. Goal-oriented

 Management focuses on achieving organizational goals.
 
2. Continuous process

 Management activities continue as long as the organization exists.
 
3. Universal activity

 Management is needed in all organizations such as businesses, hospitals, universities, 
and software companies.
 
4. Group activity

 Management coordinates the work of people working in teams.
 
5. Dynamic function

 Management must adapt to environmental and technological changes.
 
 
Functions of Management 
Management is commonly divided into four basic functions.

# Page 3

1. Planning 
Planning means deciding what to do, how to do it, when to do it, and who will do it. 
Planning helps organizations prepare for the future. 
Example: 
A software company planning to develop a mobile banking application decides: 
● features of the app
 
● development schedule
 
● required programmers
 
● project budget
 
 
2. Organizing 
Organizing means arranging resources and assigning tasks to employees.

# Page 4

It includes: 
● dividing work
 
● forming departments
 
● assigning authority and responsibility
 
Example: 
In a software company: 
● frontend developers design the interface
 
● backend developers build the server system
 
● QA engineers test the software
 
Managers organize these tasks. 
 
3. Leading (Directing) 
Leading involves guiding and motivating employees so they work effectively. 
Activities include: 
● leadership
 
● communication
 
● motivation
 
● supervision
 
Example: 
A project manager motivating developers to complete a project before deadline. 
 
4. Controlling

# Page 5

Controlling means monitoring performance and ensuring that work follows the plan. 
Steps of controlling: 
1. Setting performance standards
 
2. Measuring performance
 
3. Comparing results with standards
 
4. Taking corrective actions
 
Example: 
If a software project is delayed, the manager may adjust deadlines or add more developers.

3. Managerial Roles and Skills 
Managers perform different roles and require specific skills to perform them effectively. 
 
Managerial Roles (Henry Mintzberg) 
Mintzberg identified ten managerial roles grouped into three categories. 
 
1. Interpersonal Roles 
These roles involve interaction with employees and external parties. 
Examples: 
● Figurehead – representing the organization
 
● Leader – motivating employees
 
● Liaison – communicating with external organizations
 
Example: 
A manager representing the company in a business meeting. 
 
2. Informational Roles 
Managers gather and share information. 
Examples: 
● Monitor – collecting information
 
● Disseminator – distributing information
 
● Spokesperson – representing the organization publicly

# Page 9

Example:A manager informing employees about a new company policy. 
 
3. Decisional Roles 
Managers make decisions to solve problems and improve performance. 
Examples: 
● Entrepreneur – initiating improvements
 
● Disturbance handler – solving conflicts
 
● Resource allocator – distributing resources
 
● Negotiator – negotiating agreements
 
Example: 
A manager deciding how to allocate project budget. 
 
Managerial Skills (Robert Katz)

Examples: 
● strategic thinking

2. Human Skills 
Human skills refer to the ability to work effectively with people. 
Examples: 
● communication
 
● leadership
 
● teamwork

Human skills are essential for all managers. 
 
3. Technical Skills 
Technical skills refer to knowledge and ability to perform specialized tasks.

4. Organization and Management 
Meaning of Organization 
An organization is an association of two or more individuals formed to achieve a 
common goal. 
It refers to a group of people working together through division of labor to 
accomplish shared objectives. 
Organizations exist because many tasks cannot be effectively completed by a single 
individual and require coordinated group effort. 
Key Characteristics of Organization 
● It involves two or more people.
 
● People work together toward a common purpose.
 
● Activities are coordinated through division of labor.
 
● Resources and tasks are organized to achieve goals efficiently.
 
Examples of Organizations 
Organizations can exist in many forms such as: 
● Business organizations

# Page 12

● Social organizations
 
● Political organizations
 
● Religious organizations
 
● Clubs and associations
 
 
Meaning of Management 
The word management can be interpreted as: 
Manage – Men – T(Tactfully) 
Management refers to the optimal arrangement and utilization of 
organizational resources to achieve defined objectives. 
Management is commonly defined as: 
The art and process of getting things done through others. 
It involves managing both human resources and non-human resources in a 
coordinated and effective manner. 
Management also involves the harmonization of the following resources: 
● Men (Human resources)
 
● Money (Financial resources)
 
● Machines (Technology and tools)
 
● Materials (Physical resources)
 
● Methods (Processes and techniques)

# Page 13

By coordinating these resources effectively, management ensures the 
achievement of organizational goals. 
 
Relationship between Organization 
and Management 
Management and organization are closely interrelated concepts. 
Key Points of Relationship 
● Management runs the organization.
 
● Managers act as the drivers, while the organization functions as the 
engine.
 
● Without management, an organization may experience chaos and 
disorder.
 
● Organizations require managerial skills to function effectively.
 
● Organization provides the structural backbone for management activities.
 
● Management plays an important role in designing and structuring 
organizations.
 
● The connection between management and organization 
contributes significantly to:
 
○ efficiency
 
○ productivity

# Page 14

○ organizational success
 
● Both management and organization are interdependent and 
cannot function effectively without each other.
