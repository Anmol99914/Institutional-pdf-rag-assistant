Help / About

How to use the Institutional Knowledge Assistant.

Finding information

  1. Type a question in Ask a Question and select Ask.
  2. Read the generated answer.
  3. Under Document Sources, select View Page to open the original PDF at the page the answer was drawn from.

About the answers

Answers are generated only from the documents that have been indexed. If the documents do not contain the information, the assistant may not be able to answer. Check important details against the source page.

Complete questions work better than single keywords or acronyms.

Administration

Upload Document and Processed Documents are for maintaining the document collection. They are separated from the question workspace but are not access-controlled.
